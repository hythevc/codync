## Risk register

Tiger means an evidenced problem or material acceptance gap. Paper Tiger means a concern overstated relative to the implementation or approved scope, not proof of safety. Elephant means an uncomfortable assumption that needs explicit examination. Launch-blocking requires evidence or an owner resolution before selection; fast-follow belongs after the required launch gates; track retains a bounded uncertainty.
